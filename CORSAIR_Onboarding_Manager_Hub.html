01 — Introduction
Why Onboarding Matters

This guide gives you an overview of the Onboarding Program, describes your role, and provides support materials to make your job easier. Your commitment to this program is what will make it successful.

Proper engagement of a new hire in the first 90 days of employment offers three key advantages:

Morale
New hires develop positive impressions of their work environment, company, and peers.
Retention
Employees feel more secure in their role and are less likely to question joining the team.
Productivity
New hires learn and perform their duties more quickly, reducing time to competency.

04 — Week 1 Onboarding
Day One Priorities

After orientation, help your new hire get familiar with their immediate work environment. A strong first day sets the tone for their entire experience.

Daily touch point required. Plan to spend at least 30 minutes each day with your new hire during their first week. This is one of the most impactful things you can do.
01
Show them their desk and workstation setup
02
Introduce the location of team members and adjacent teams
03
Walk through conference room locations and reservation process
04
Point out restrooms, cafeteria, break room, gym, and exits
05
Take them to lunch — keep it relaxed and welcoming
05 — Week 2 & Beyond
Building Momentum

Schedule a structured department overview and begin stakeholder introductions. Work with the mentor to maintain daily engagement.

▸ CORSAIR org structure and your department's role
▸ Department structure, active projects, and business impact
▸ How to schedule meetings in Outlook/Teams
▸ How to set up calendar reminders and OOO messages
▸ Confirm daily check-ins with mentor are happening
▸ Check in on questions, blockers, and overall progress
Check-in cadence: Conduct manager check-ins monthly at minimum through Day 90.

01 — Introduction
Remote Onboarding Considerations

This guide provides considerations for onboarding Remote New Hires, whose activities may differ from those of an onsite new hire. The information is guidance only and should be applied as appropriate to meet business needs.

This guide may be used by HR Representatives, Managers, and Mentors, and is organized around two key phases:

Pre-Hire
Administrative tasks before the start date, with additional steps specific to remote workers.
Week 1+
Activities spanning the full employment phase, with strategies to keep remote hires engaged.
Remote First
Working from home can be isolating — intentional connection strategies make a big difference.

04 — Week 1 & Beyond
Remote Engagement Strategies

Onboarding activities during the first week should involve a variety of personnel to give the remote new hire as many connections as possible. Consider having people beyond the mentor take part — such as showing them different systems and software.

▸ Involve multiple people in Week 1 — not just the mentor
▸ Review travel policy and how to submit expenses early on
▸ Arrange participation in stakeholder meetings where possible
▸ Establish team communication norms and webcam expectations
▸ Discuss schedule coverage and email/voicemail check frequency
▸ Remote employees may visit the worksite for key meetings
Daily touch point required. Plan to spend at least 30 minutes each day with your new hire during their first week — virtually via Teams or a call.
