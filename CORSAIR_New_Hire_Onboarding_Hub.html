01 — Welcome
Welcome to CORSAIR!

Welcome to the Onboarding Program for new hires. We're very excited to have you join us! This guide is designed to give you an overview of the Onboarding Program, describe the role you play, and provide assistance with support materials to make your transition go well.

— The CORSAIR Team

Message from the Chief People and Consumer Experience Officer
We are thrilled to have you join our team at CORSAIR. Everyone on our team plays an important role. We hope you find your experience with us inspiring and rewarding. We're excited to work with you and see what great things we can accomplish together!

— Pete Hilliard
02 — Significance of Onboarding
Your First 90 Days

Your first 90 days are a formative time at CORSAIR. Our onboarding program provides you with the information, resources, and connections you need to be successful long-term.

Learn the Culture
We can't wait to introduce you to your new work environment, company, and peers.
Meet Your Team
Our onboarding includes touchpoints with key contacts so you can build relationships quickly.
Learn Your Role
Our program provides clear information about expectations to help you make a difference right away.
03 — The Onboarding Team
Who's Supporting You
Manager
Your Manager
Has been preparing for your arrival. In your first 30 days, your manager will:
▸ Orient you to your team and key projects
▸ Define expectations and responsibilities
▸ Help you identify and schedule team meetings
HR
HR Representative
Will cover new hire orientation including company history, culture, and benefits, and give you an office tour.
Mentor
Your Mentor
Your main day-to-day point of contact throughout your first weeks.
▸ Available to answer questions
▸ Introduces you to team and key systems

06 — Week 2 & Beyond
Building Momentum

Congratulations on completing your first week! In Week 2 and beyond, you'll continue receiving guidance and support. The key part of this phase is stakeholder introduction meetings.

Stakeholder Introduction Meetings: Starting Week 2, you may participate in introductory meetings with individuals outside your immediate team. Your manager and mentor will help you identify and schedule these.
