01 — Welcome
Welcome to CORSAIR!

Welcome to the Onboarding Program for new hires. We're very excited to have you join us! This guide is designed to give you an overview of the Onboarding Program, describe the role you play, and provide assistance with support materials to make your transition go well.

— The CORSAIR Team

Message from the Chief People and Consumer Experience Officer
We are thrilled to have you join our team at CORSAIR. Everyone on our team plays an important role. We hope you find your experience with us inspiring and rewarding. We're excited to work with you and see what great things we can accomplish together!

— Pete Hilliard
02 — Significance of Onboarding
Why Onboarding Matters

The first 90 days are critical to your success at CORSAIR. A structured onboarding process helps you:

Get Oriented
Learn about CORSAIR's culture, operations, and your role within the organization.
Build Connections
Meet your teammates, mentors, and leaders who will support your growth.
Accelerate Success
Quickly develop competency in your role and contribute meaningful work.
03 — The Onboarding Team
Your Support System

You're not going through this alone. Meet the people who will help you succeed:

Manager
Your Manager
Your primary resource for role clarity, expectations, and career development.
Mentor
Your Mentor
Your day-to-day guide who knows the systems, processes, and people you need to know.
HR
HR Representative
Your resource for benefits, policies, and company programs.
04 — Week 1 Activities
Your First Week

Expect to participate in orientation, meet your team, and begin learning your role. Make the most of it:

Orientation
Company overview, benefits enrollment, facilities tour, and IT setup.
Meet Your Team
Introductions to direct reports, peers, and cross-functional partners.
Role Overview
Understand expectations, priorities, and initial projects you'll work on.

Settling In

01 — Welcome
Welcome to Remote CORSAIR

Welcome to CORSAIR! Whether working from home is new to you or you're an experienced remote worker, we're here to help you succeed. This guide provides tips and resources specific to remote onboarding.

Remote work at CORSAIR means flexibility, autonomy, and the opportunity to be part of a collaborative, global team—all from the comfort of your home.

02 — Home Office Setup
Creating Your Workspace

A proper home office setup is essential for productivity and comfort:

Equipment Provided
▸ Laptop & Docking Station
▸ Monitor
▸ Keyboard & Mouse
▸ Headset
Setup Best Practices
▸ Ergonomic desk & chair
▸ Good lighting
▸ Quiet workspace
▸ Stable internet
03 — Technology & Tools
Staying Connected

Familiarize yourself with the tools you'll use daily:

Communication
Slack for messaging, Teams for video calls, and Email for formal communication.
Collaboration
OneDrive for file sharing, Sharepoint for team resources, and Jira for project tracking.
IT Support
IT Help Desk available via email or Slack for technical assistance and troubleshooting.
04 — First Week Tips
Making the Most of Week 1
Tip: Treat your first week like you're in an office. Get up early, dress professionally, and maintain regular working hours to establish good routines.
Do's
✓ Join all scheduled meetings on camera
✓ Take detailed notes
✓ Ask questions early
✓ Build relationships with teammates
Don'ts
✗ Get overwhelmed by information overload
✗ Skip virtual coffee chats
✗ Work in isolation
✗ Turn off your camera in meetings
05 — Staying Connected
Remote Culture

Building strong connections remotely takes intentionality:

Virtual Coffee Chats
Schedule informal 15-minute calls with teammates to build relationships.
Team Events
Participate in virtual team meetings, happy hours, and company-wide events.
Communication Channels
Use Slack channels to stay in the loop and engage with your team daily.

Final Thoughts
Welcome to Our Remote Team

Remote work at CORSAIR offers flexibility and opportunity. By setting up your space properly, using our tools effectively, and staying connected with your team, you'll thrive from day one. We look forward to your contributions and are excited to have you on our team.
